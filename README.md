# 📌 Problem Statement

## PS1 — Digital Estate & Financial Closure Assistant for Grieving Families

When someone dies, their family may inherit a complex financial closure process involving:

- Forgotten insurance policies
- Unclaimed EPF / PPF accounts
- Active loans and EMIs
- Auto-renewing subscriptions
- Bank accounts and fixed deposits
- Investments and demat holdings
- Nominee and documentation issues

These financial relationships are distributed across different institutions and platforms, making it difficult for families to understand the complete financial picture and determine what needs to be done next.

### Objective

Develop a practical solution that:

- Improves clarity and organization around financial responsibilities and information.
- Helps families identify relevant concerns and understand their next steps.
- Supports different stages of the financial closure process.

---

# 💡 Our Solution — CLAIM SAATHI

**CLAIM SAATHI** is a digital estate and financial closure assistant designed to help families organize the financial responsibilities left behind after the loss of a loved one.

The platform brings financial information, documents, liabilities and required actions into a structured workspace.

### Core Workflow

> **Triage → Discover → Verify → Organize → Act → Track**

CLAIM SAATHI focuses on helping families understand:

**What exists? → What needs attention? → What documents are required? → What should be done next? → What has already been completed?**

# 🚀 Key Features

## 1. Guided Intake & Asset Discovery

A structured triage process helps create an initial financial estate profile.

The intake can capture:

- Deceased person's basic information
- Claimant information
- Employment context
- Known financial institutions
- Known assets
- Known liabilities
- Nominee / will-related information

The system then organizes the available information into an estate workspace.

---

## 2. Financial Estate Overview

CLAIM SAATHI provides a centralized view of the financial estate.

### Assets

- Bank accounts
- Fixed deposits
- EPF / PPF
- NPS / pension
- Mutual funds
- Demat / stocks
- Insurance
- Other financial assets

### Liabilities

- Home loans
- Personal loans
- Vehicle loans
- Education loans
- Credit cards
- Active EMIs

---

## 3. First 30-Day Playbook

CLAIM SAATHI organizes financial closure activities into practical stages.

### Days 1–7
Immediate documentation and financial review.

### Days 8–30
Institutional claims and account-related actions.

### Day 30+
Longer-term closure and unclaimed-asset discovery.

The playbook provides a structured checklist so that important actions are less likely to be overlooked.

---

## 4. Claim Packs & Document Assistance

The platform organizes claim-related information and document requirements for relevant financial processes.

Examples include:

- Bank-related claim guidance
- EPFO-related claim guidance
- EDLI-related guidance
- Insurance claim documentation
- Supporting document checklists

The system can also provide printable/structured drafts where appropriate.

> CLAIM SAATHI is a guidance and organization layer and does not replace the official process of the relevant institution.

---

## 5. Financial Risk & Liability Shield

CLAIM SAATHI helps identify outstanding liabilities and recurring financial obligations.

Examples:

- Active loan EMIs
- NACH / ECS-related repayments
- Credit card obligations
- Other recurring financial commitments

The platform can generate notification/request drafts for communication with relevant institutions.

Users can also be prompted to check whether applicable loan-protection or insurance coverage exists.

---

## 6. Unclaimed Asset Discovery

CLAIM SAATHI provides guided pathways for discovering potentially unclaimed financial assets.

The prototype includes guidance for systems such as:

- RBI UDGAM
- IEPF
- EPFO-related processes

The application does not claim direct access to institutional databases unless an authorized integration is actually implemented.

---
